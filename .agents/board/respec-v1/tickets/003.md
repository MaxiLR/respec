## Goal

Make switching safe against interruption.

## Acceptance criteria

- A switch is journaled in `state.json` (planned removals/installs and progress) before mutating anything.
- If a switch is interrupted (simulated failure mid-way), the next respec command detects the incomplete journal and offers/performs resume or rollback, ending in a consistent state (exactly one profile's items present).
- `status` reports an incomplete switch clearly.

## Testing seam

Inject a failure at each step of the switch (fault hook or monkeypatched filesystem op) and assert recovery leads to a consistent filesystem and state.

## Exclusions

Concurrent respec processes beyond a simple lock file.

## Context

Spec risk "Interrupted switch".

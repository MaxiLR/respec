## Goal

`respec restore [<backup>]` to undo respec.

## Acceptance criteria

- Lists available backups; restores the chosen (default: earliest takeover) backup for each target it contains, after removing respec-managed links.
- Restored folders match backup contents exactly; respec releases ownership (state cleared, no active profile).
- Refuses to overwrite unknown entries without the adopt flow or explicit confirmation.

## Testing seam

Take over populated fake folders, switch profiles, restore; compare folder trees with original fixtures.
